## Vegetarian and vegan diets and vitamin B12
Vitamin B12 occurs mainly in animal foods. People following vegetarian or vegan diets may benefit from a B12 supplement or fortified foods. Blood tests are the only reliable way to check B12 status.

## Vitamin D and sun exposure
People who work indoors or have limited sun exposure may have lower vitamin D levels. A doctor can advise whether testing is needed.

## Sleep and magnesium
Magnesium contributes to normal muscle function. Evidence that magnesium improves sleep is mixed, so it should be described as supportive, not as a sleep treatment.
